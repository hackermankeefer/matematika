The Mathematics Archive
An interactive Grade 11 General Mathematics learning website designed to make mathematics easier to understand, explore, and practice.
About
The Mathematics Archive combines curriculum-based lessons, worked examples, formulas, visualizations, and an AI-powered math tutor in one platform.
The website is designed to help students not only find the correct answer, but also understand the process and reasoning behind the solution.
Features
- 📚 Lessons — Explore Grade 11 General Mathematics topics and concepts.
- 🤖 AI Math Tutor — Ask questions, request explanations, check your work, and get help solving problems.
- 🧮 Mathematical Calculator — Perform and verify mathematical calculations.
- 📐 Formula Support — View and understand important formulas with properly formatted mathematical notation.
- 📊 Visualizations — Explore mathematical concepts through graphs and interactive visual elements.
- ✏️ Worked Examples — Follow step-by-step solutions to mathematical problems.
- 🔍 Explore — Discover formulas, examples, and additional mathematical concepts.
