Curriculum Topics

Explore the six core areas of Grade 11 mathematics. Each topic builds upon foundational concepts and prepares you for advanced mathematical thinking.

Functions

Understand how quantities relate and change together

  • Function notation and evaluation
  • Domain and range
  • Linear, quadratic, and exponential functions
  • Function transformations
  • Inverse functions
  • Composite functions
12 lessons ~8 hours

Algebra

Master symbolic manipulation and equation solving

  • Polynomial operations
  • Factoring techniques
  • Rational expressions
  • Solving equations and inequalities
  • Systems of equations
  • Complex numbers
14 lessons ~10 hours

Trigonometry

Explore relationships between angles and sides

  • Unit circle and radian measure
  • Trigonometric ratios
  • Trigonometric identities
  • Graphs of trig functions
  • Solving trigonometric equations
  • Law of sines and cosines
11 lessons ~9 hours

Geometry

Study shapes, spaces, and their properties

  • Coordinate geometry
  • Lines and slopes
  • Circles and conic sections
  • Geometric proofs
  • Transformations
  • 3D geometry basics
10 lessons ~7 hours

Statistics

Learn to collect, analyze, and interpret data

  • Measures of central tendency
  • Measures of spread
  • Data visualization
  • Normal distribution
  • Correlation and regression
  • Statistical inference basics
9 lessons ~6 hours

Probability

Quantify uncertainty and predict outcomes

  • Probability fundamentals
  • Compound events
  • Conditional probability
  • Permutations and combinations
  • Binomial probability
  • Expected value
8 lessons ~6 hours

Suggested Learning Path

Follow this sequence for optimal understanding and skill development

1

Functions

Start here to build a strong foundation in mathematical relationships

2

3

Trigonometry

4

Geometry

Visualize and prove relationships in space

5

Statistics & Probability

Apply mathematical thinking to real-world data and uncertainty
